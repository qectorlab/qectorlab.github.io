Activating your license

Everyone installs the same wheel: pip install --upgrade "qector-decoder-v3>=1.0,<2". If QECTOR_LICENSE is unset a licensing notice prints on import, which is expected for non-commercial use. Set both QECTOR_LICENSE and QECTOR_LICENSE_KEY to the same token. Every paid plan unlocks GPU (CUDA/OpenCL) batch decoding; CPU decoding is never blocked, and distance caps apply only with QECTOR_ENFORCE=1.

# Commercial use: activate with the Ed25519 token from your licence email
export QECTOR_LICENSE=""
export QECTOR_LICENSE_KEY=""

# Optional: suppress the licensing notice in CI logs
export QECTOR_SILENT=1

# Verification is offline against a public key in the package.
# No licence server, no phone-home, works air-gapped.
python -c "import qector_decoder_v3 as q; print(q.get_license_info()['tier'])"

Frequently asked questions

How do I try QECTOR commercially before paying?

Request the free 14-day commercial trial on this page: a Pro licence token arrives by e-mail within minutes, for up to 5 internal users. No card is needed and the token simply expires; there is nothing to cancel. One trial per e-mail address per year.

How is the licence delivered, and what does the token unlock?

By e-mail within minutes of payment (check spam, then write to admin@qector.store with your Stripe receipt). Everyone installs the same wheel: pip install --upgrade "qector-decoder-v3>=1.0,<2". Set QECTOR_LICENSE_KEY and QECTOR_LICENSE to the token. Every paid plan unlocks GPU (CUDA/OpenCL) batch decoding; Lab and Enterprise also raise the code-distance tier to 63. CPU decoding is never blocked, and distance caps are only enforced if you set QECTOR_ENFORCE=1. The token is verified offline against a public key in the package: no licence server, works air-gapped.

Do plans renew automatically, and how do I cancel?

Yes. Pro (monthly or annual), Team and Lab renew automatically until cancelled, and you get a reminder e-mail 14 days before each annual renewal. Cancel any time from your Stripe receipt or by writing to admin@qector.store; your licence stays valid until the end of the paid period. Pro Perpetual and the Pilot are one-time payments and never renew.

What is your refund policy?

14-day money-back guarantee on your first payment of any plan: write to admin@qector.store with your Stripe receipt and we refund it in full and revoke the token. Renewals are not refunded pro rata. Duplicate purchases and charges made in error are always refunded. If a token never arrives or fails to verify, we reissue it at no cost. This does not limit non-waivable statutory rights.

How many seats does each plan cover, and what counts as production?

Pro: 1 named user. Team: up to 10. Lab: up to 25. Enterprise: unlimited. All paid plans grant production rights for internal use. The free trial and the 60-day Pilot are for evaluation (benchmarking, integration testing, threshold studies), not production. Pro Perpetual covers one named user for every 3.x release; a major version such as 4.0 is a new licence.

Is a hosted or customer-facing API covered?

No. Pro, Team and Lab cover internal use on your own infrastructure. As soon as QECTOR sits behind an API, product or service that anyone outside your organisation can reach, that is SaaS or redistribution and needs an Enterprise/OEM agreement. Internal endpoints used only by your own staff are fine. Unsure? Describe the deployment to admin@qector.store.

Can I redistribute QECTOR inside my product?

Only under an Enterprise/OEM agreement, which covers redistribution, SaaS hosting and hardware bundling. Write to admin@qector.store.

Do I need a licence for non-commercial research?

No. Personal, academic and other non-commercial use is free under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. Universities, public research organisations, charities and government institutions may also use it free, whatever the source of their funding (that is what PolyForm Noncommercial says). A company using QECTOR, including on a contract with a university, needs a paid plan. Academic institutions that want a paid plan anyway, for support or to pass commercial rights to an industry partner, get 40% off Team and Lab with an institutional e-mail.

What currency are prices in, and is tax included?

Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax. Applicable sales tax, GST/HST or VAT is calculated and added at checkout from your billing address. Businesses can enter a VAT/GST number at checkout.

Can we get a signed agreement, tax form or security questionnaire?

Yes. For a signed PDF agreement, vendor profile, W-8BEN or equivalent, or a security questionnaire, write to admin@qector.store with your Stripe invoice number. Enterprise agreements are always signed contracts.
