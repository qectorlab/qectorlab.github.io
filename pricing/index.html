Activating Your License

Everyone installs the same wheel from PyPI: there is no separate commercial build. Your token unlocks the paid features: GPU (CUDA/OpenCL) batch decoding on every paid plan, and code distances up to 63 on Lab and Enterprise. CPU decoding is never blocked; distance caps are enforced only when you set QECTOR_ENFORCE=1.

pip install --upgrade "qector-decoder-v3>=1.0.1,<2"
export QECTOR_LICENSE_KEY="<your-token>"
export QECTOR_LICENSE="<your-token>"

# Verify offline: prints Pro or Enterprise
python -c "import qector_decoder_v3 as q; print(q.get_license_info()['tier'])"

Tokens are verified offline against a public key embedded in the package: no license server, no phone-home, works completely air-gapped.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does licensing work on air-gapped machines?

+

Yes. A licence is a signed token verified offline against a public key built into the package. There is no licence server, no activation step and no network call, so it works on isolated and air-gapped systems.

How is the licence delivered, and what does the token unlock?

+

By e-mail within minutes of payment (check spam, then write to admin@qector.store with your Stripe receipt). Everyone installs the same wheel: pip install --upgrade "qector-decoder-v3>=1.0.1,<2". Set QECTOR_LICENSE_KEY and QECTOR_LICENSE to the token. Every paid plan unlocks GPU (CUDA/OpenCL) batch decoding; Lab and Enterprise also raise the code-distance tier to 63. CPU decoding is never blocked, and distance caps are only enforced if you set QECTOR_ENFORCE=1.

Can I try it before committing?

+

Every plan carries a 14-day money-back guarantee on the first payment: buy Pro monthly, run it on your own workloads, and if it is not right write to admin@qector.store for a full refund. For a structured evaluation with an integration call, take the 60-day Pilot, which is fully creditable toward Lab.

Do plans renew automatically, and how do I cancel?

+

Yes. Pro (monthly or annual) and Lab renew automatically until cancelled, with a reminder e-mail 14 days before each annual renewal. Cancel any time from your Stripe receipt or by writing to admin@qector.store; your licence stays valid until the end of the paid period. Pro Perpetual and the Pilot are one-time payments and never renew.

What is your refund policy?

+

14-day money-back guarantee on your first payment of any plan: write to admin@qector.store with your Stripe receipt and we refund it in full. Renewals are not refunded pro rata. Duplicate purchases and charges made in error are always refunded. If a token never arrives or fails to verify, we reissue it at no cost. This does not limit non-waivable statutory rights.

What does a licence cover?

+

Internal commercial use by your organisation, including production. Pro and Lab differ in licence tier (Lab raises the code-distance tier to 63) and support level. Pro Perpetual covers every 1.x release of qector-decoder-v3; a new major version such as 2.0 is a new licence. The Pilot is for evaluation (benchmarking, integration testing, threshold studies), not production.

Is a hosted or customer-facing API covered?

+

No. Pro and Lab cover internal use on your own infrastructure. As soon as QECTOR sits behind an API, product or service that anyone outside your organisation can reach, that is SaaS or redistribution and needs an Enterprise/OEM agreement. Internal endpoints used only by your own staff are fine. Unsure? Describe the deployment to admin@qector.store.

Can I redistribute QECTOR inside my product?

+

Only under an Enterprise/OEM agreement, which covers redistribution, SaaS hosting and hardware bundling. Write to admin@qector.store.

Do I need a licence for non-commercial research?

+

No. Personal, academic and other non-commercial use is free under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. Universities, public research organisations, charities and government institutions may also use it free, whatever the source of their funding. A company using QECTOR, including on a contract with a university, needs a paid plan. Academic institutions that want a paid plan anyway, for support or to pass commercial rights to an industry partner, get 40% off Lab with an institutional e-mail.

What currency are prices in, and is tax included?

+

Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax. Applicable sales tax, GST/QST or VAT is calculated and added at checkout from your billing address. Businesses can enter a VAT/GST number at checkout.

Can we get a signed agreement, tax form or security questionnaire?

+

Yes. For a signed PDF agreement, vendor profile, W-8BEN or equivalent, or a security questionnaire, write to admin@qector.store with your Stripe invoice number. Enterprise agreements are always signed contracts.

What is not included: Unless explicitly granted, no tier includes redistribution, OEM, SaaS hosting, or sublicensing rights. Contact sales for custom terms.

Procurement: All prices are in USD; applicable taxes are added at checkout. Monthly and annual plans renew automatically until cancelled, with a reminder before each annual renewal. 14-day money-back guarantee on your first payment of any plan. Tokens are emailed within minutes.
