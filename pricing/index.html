Frequently asked questions

How is the license delivered?

Automatically, by email, within minutes of payment. You receive an Ed25519-signed license token tied to your checkout email — set it as QECTOR_LICENSE and it verifies offline against a public key embedded in the package, so it works air-gapped and in CI with no license server and no phone-home. There are no software lockouts: your team keeps using the standard pip install qector-decoder-v3 package as-is, and the token plus your Stripe receipt are what procurement and audit need.

What happens after the 60-day evaluation expires?

The $499 evaluation is a flat, non-recurring 60-day window. If you need to extend or convert to an annual license, contact licensing@qector.store. The evaluation fee is 100% creditable toward any annual license.

Can I redistribute QECTOR inside my product?

No — standard tiers cover internal use only. Enterprise OEM licenses cover redistribution, SaaS hosting, and hardware bundling. Email licensing@qector.store for a custom agreement.

Do I need a license for non-commercial research?

No. Non-commercial, academic, and personal use is free under the PolyForm Noncommercial license. Only commercial deployment requires a paid tier.

What about academic discounts?

Yes — academic institutions receive 40% off any annual tier. Contact licensing@qector.store with your .edu domain to verify.

Can we get a signed corporate EULA or tax form?

Yes. If procurement requires a signed PDF agreement, vendor profile, or W-8/W-9, email your request with your Stripe invoice number to licensing@qector.store.

Contact: licensing@qector.store
