14-day money-back guarantee

If QECTOR is not right for you, e-mail admin@qector.store with your Stripe receipt within 14 days of your first payment of any plan (Pro monthly or annual, Pro Perpetual, Lab or the Pilot). We refund it in full, no questions asked, and revoke the licence token.

  • One guarantee per organisation; it applies to the first purchase, not to later renewals.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
  • Once refunded, the licence ends and the token must no longer be used.

Renewals and cancelling

Pro (monthly or annual) and Lab are subscriptions and renew automatically until cancelled. We e-mail a reminder 14 days before each annual renewal. Cancel at any time in the customer portal (sign in with your billing e-mail) or by e-mailing admin@qector.store; your licence stays valid until the end of the period you paid for.

Renewal payments are not refunded pro rata. If a renewal went through that you meant to cancel and you have not used the renewed term, write to us within 7 days and we will refund it. Pro Perpetual and the Pilot are one-time payments and never renew.

Delivery problems: we fix them

If your token never arrives, arrives corrupted, is tied to the wrong e-mail or fails offline verification, that is a delivery fault on our side. Do not open a dispute: e-mail admin@qector.store with your Stripe receipt and we will reissue or correct it at no cost, with no time limit.

Tokens are e-mailed within minutes of payment, usually under 10 minutes. If it has been longer, check your spam folder first.

Duplicate and mistaken charges

Accidental duplicate purchases of the same plan for the same organisation, and charges made in obvious error, are always refunded in full. E-mail admin@qector.store with both receipts within 30 days of the charge.

Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot be waived under the consumer protection law that applies to you, including the Québec Consumer Protection Act where it applies.

Currency and tax

All prices are quoted and charged in US dollars (USD), exclusive of tax. Any sales tax, GST/QST or VAT is calculated at checkout from your billing address and refunded with the price. Businesses can enter a tax number at checkout.
